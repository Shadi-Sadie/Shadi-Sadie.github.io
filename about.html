Shadi Seyedi, PhD

I am a researcher interested in immigration, belonging, and health, as well as in how policies, institutions, and communities shape people's everyday experiences.

I am trained in quantitative policy evaluation and causal inference and work across disciplines and formats, including health data analysis, writing, teaching, data visualization, and interactive storytelling.

I am especially drawn to questions that sit between categories and resist simple answers, and I care not only about what research finds but also about how it is shared. That care isn't abstract for me. I came to these questions through both my own life and my research. I was born and raised in Iran and now live in the US — neither fully here nor fully there, yet somehow both. That experience has shaped many of the questions I return to in my work on migration, identity, and belonging.
